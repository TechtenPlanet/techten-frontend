🕷️ Meet the Mascot

Robot Ananse —
Ghana's Cleverest Spider

Inspired by the legendary Ghanaian trickster Kweku Ananse, Robot Ananse is a tiny spider-robot with a massive brain. He appears in every issue as your child's guide — cracking puzzles, starring in the comic strip, and teaching computer thinking through storytelling.

He is mischievous, curious, and always three steps ahead — just like the best learners.

❓ FAQ

Common Questions

What age is the Techten Book for?

The book is designed for children aged 6–12. We have two reading levels: Junior (6–9) and Explorer (10–12). All content is written to be accessible even to younger readers with a little parent help.

Does my child need a computer to use it?

No — the book is fully offline. The "Think Like a Computer" section is designed as an unplugged activity. Everything is on paper, including puzzles, writing, and the coding challenges.

What language is it written in?

The Techten Book is written in clear, simple English. Future editions may include Twi or other Ghanaian language versions as demand grows.

Can I order for multiple children?

Yes! Register each child separately using the form above. For families with 3 or more children, contact us for a family discount.

How do I pay for my subscription?

After registering, we will contact you within 24 hours via WhatsApp or phone with Mobile Money (MoMo) or bank transfer details. Monthly or annual payment options are available.

Can schools order in bulk?

Absolutely — we welcome school orders. Select "School Bulk Order" in the form or email us directly at admin@techtenplanet.org for custom school pricing and delivery arrangements.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, with one month's notice. Just contact us via WhatsApp or email before the next billing cycle and we will process your cancellation with no penalty.

Is there a digital version?

Not yet, but Explorer Bundle subscribers receive a QR code in each book linking to an optional digital bonus activity. A full digital version is planned for 2027.
